Bind an xAI account to a workspace. `Grok: Bind Account to This Workspace` pins the current repository to a signed-in profile, and the resource scoped `grokCopilot.profile` and `grokCopilot.profileMap` settings do the same by hand. Folder and workspace bindings are only honoured in a trusted workspace, and `grokCopilot.workspaceBinding` controls that.
